How to use the engine

You cut reels for several clients in Claude Code; this engine is Claude's toolbelt and rulebook. This page is the map. The chat is where the editing happens.

The road, every reel

1 · Pick the brandon this page, or "switch to acme"
2 · Drop the clipinto inbox/
3 · "edit this reel"transcribe, cut, stitch, level
⏸ you review
4 · "plan the graphics"register and finish asked here
⏸ approve
5 · "beat sheet"numbered stills; send to the client
⏸ approve
6 · "build it"render once → MP4 or CapCut

Nothing about the reel is asked before the cut. Every ⏸ is one approval; after the build there are no more creative choices.

Brands: how a client's voice gets into a reel

Each client is a folder under brands/: their brand-kit.md, their style packs, their licensed fonts, their learned preferences and their reels. Agency defaults is the layer every client inherits from.

Any setting resolves brand → agency → engine default. A client with no pack of their own gets the agency's; a client with no kit yet gets agency defaults and a nudge to fill theirs. One client's voice never reaches another's reel.

The chips on a brand's card tell you what is still missing: kit fields blank, no fonts, no pack of their own.

The rules Claude works by

  1. Plan, approve, build once. It never builds off a guess.
  2. Transcribe once; the cut script is truth. Fix a word there and it fixes everywhere.
  3. Cut by transcript, clean by sound. No boundary is ever trimmed by eye.
  4. Measure, never eyeball: text is placed from where the speaker's head actually is.
  5. A check warns, never blocks. You always get the reel, plus one plain line about anything flagged.
  6. One layer per change. Changing one graphic never re-renders the reel.
  7. A style pack is config. Fonts, colours, sizes come from the brand's pack by role.
  8. Never replace a CapCut draft; versions go 1.1 → 1.2, and CapCut is quit for every write.
  9. Every on-screen word is the client's, in the client's language. Nothing invented.
  10. Nothing leaves this computer.

When something goes wrong

Claude asks which brand every time.
No brand is active. Press "Make active" here, or say "switch to <brand>".
The hook or captions came out in the wrong voice.
Check the active brand and its kit. The kit is the only source of voice; fill it before cutting.
Text sits on the speaker's face.
Say where you want it ("captions above the head"), then "remember that" so it sticks for this brand.
A render hangs or crawls.
Say "/report-a-problem". Never stop a render that is still counting frames.
Fonts look wrong.
The brand's licensed fonts go in brands/<brand>/fonts/. The Neutral pack's fonts go in assets/fonts/.
I want the designer's eye on it before the client.
The beat sheet is the hand-off: one numbered image, notes by beat number, before anything renders.
